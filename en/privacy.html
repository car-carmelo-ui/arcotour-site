The app

In short

ArcoTour has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Your location stays on your phone: the GPS is processed only there, and we do not receive it, we do not save it and we do not send it to anyone.

The app uses the internet for only two things: downloading maps from OpenStreetMap and, if you buy a pass, talking to Apple’s App Store. Map requests let OpenStreetMap see your IP address and the area of the map you are looking at, which may roughly include where you are (see “Maps”). The stories, the texts and the photos are inside the app.

Who we are and why we process data

Data controller: Carmelo Albanese, Via Montessori n. 22, 89010 Terranova Sappo Minulio (RC), Italia.

For any question about privacy, write to supporto.arcotour@gmail.com.

Why we process data, and on what legal basis (Article 6 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the GDPR):

Your phone’s location, for the tours: with your consent, which you give through the iOS permission (point (a)).

The maps and the Rome passes: to provide the service you ask for by using the app (point (b)).

The messages you write to us: to reply to you, at your request (point (b)), or for our legitimate interest in answering people who write to us (point (f)).

Legal obligations: if the law requires us to keep some data (point (c)).

Who receives it: the app’s data does not reach us. OpenStreetMap (map requests) and Apple (purchases) receive it on their own behalf; the support mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail) and this website by GitHub.

Outside the European Union: Apple, Google and GitHub belong to groups based in the United States and, in their own policies, state that they use the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses for transfers of data outside the European Economic Area (GitHub also adheres to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework). The OpenStreetMap Foundation is registered in the United Kingdom, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of data protection. For us, these are the transfer safeguards provided for by the GDPR (Chapter V).

Your location

The app uses your phone’s location for three things: knowing when you reach a stop on the tour so the story can start, showing you the public transport stops nearby, and guiding you on foot to a stop.

It only uses it while a tour, the “Transport” tab or the “Take me there” feature is in use, and it keeps going with the screen off or the app in the background, so the story starts even when your phone is in your pocket. While it does, iOS shows an indicator.

Your location is processed only on your phone: it is not saved, it does not reach us and it is not shared with anyone. We do not use it for advertising or to track you.

The permission is yours to give and is optional: you can refuse or withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → ArcoTour. Without location the app still works, but the stories do not start by themselves.

Maps

The maps come from OpenStreetMap. To show them, the app downloads small map tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The tiles come from a network of servers in several countries, which the OpenStreetMap Foundation uses.

Those servers receive your IP address, the tiles you ask for (that is, the area of the map you are looking at, which may roughly include where you are: for example when “Take me there” frames the route from your position to the stop), the time of the request and some technical information, such as the name of the app. They do not receive your name or your GPS coordinates.

OpenStreetMap may record this data: its own policy lists IP address, browser and device type, operating system, date and time and pages accessed, which it uses to run and protect the service and for anonymised statistics. It handles them on its own behalf: we neither receive nor see them. An IP address, however, is personal data. How long it keeps them is up to OpenStreetMap, not us; how it handles them is explained in its own policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.

For this reason, in the App Store privacy information we declare, as a precaution, “approximate location” as data linked to you, used only to make the maps work: we do not use it for advertising or to track you.

The app keeps a temporary copy of the tiles you have already seen on your phone, so you can see them again without internet.

Purchases

The Rome passes are bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple account: we neither see nor keep your card number or any other payment data, and we do not receive your name or the email of your Apple account. How Apple handles your data is explained in its own policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

The app receives the purchase confirmation from Apple and keeps on your phone only which passes you have and until when they are valid, to unlock the content. “Restore purchases” asks Apple to confirm your purchases again and needs internet.

What stays on your phone

The language you chose, the state of your passes (with a time reference to check when they expire) and a temporary copy of the map tiles. Nothing else.

This data stays on your phone until you remove the app: removing it deletes it. There is no archive on any server of ours.

What we do not do

There is no account to create. There are no ads. We do not use cookies, analytics, statistics or tracking tools, nor third-party libraries that collect data about how you use the app.

We do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.

We do not sell or hand over data: the app’s data does not reach us. The typefaces are inside the app: they are not downloaded from outside services.

Your rights

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) gives you the right to ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to have its processing restricted, to receive it in a portable format and to object to the processing. You can also withdraw at any time the permissions you have given, such as location, from iOS Settings.

The app has no accounts and its data does not reach us: the only personal data we may have is that of the message, if you write to us. We keep support messages for up to 24 months after the request is closed, unless the law requires us to keep them for longer. To exercise your rights write to supporto.arcotour@gmail.com: we reply without undue delay and in any case within one month.

For the data that OpenStreetMap (map requests) and Apple (purchases) handle on their own behalf, you exercise your rights with them, using the contacts given in their own policies: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy and apple.com/legal/privacy.

If you think your data has been handled incorrectly you can write to us at supporto.arcotour@gmail.com and you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country (in Italy, the Garante: garanteprivacy.it).

Changes

If in the future the app uses data in a different way (for example with an account or with downloadable maps), we will update this page and the date below, and we will say so in the app before we start.

This website

Cookies, browsing data and email

This website does not use cookies, has no analytics or tracking tools and does not load fonts, images or scripts from other services: everything you see is hosted here.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). As GitHub explains, when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored by GitHub for security purposes, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account. We do not use this data. How GitHub handles it is explained in its privacy statement and on the page What is GitHub Pages?.

If you email us, we use your address and the content of your message only to reply to you.
