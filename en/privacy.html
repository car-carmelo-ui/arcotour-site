The app

In short

ArcoTour has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

GPS coordinates stay on the device. To display the maps, OpenStreetMap receives the technical data of the request and the requested map area, which may approximately indicate the area where the user is located.

We do not receive the GPS coordinates, we do not save them and we do not send them to anyone. The app uses the internet for only two things: downloading maps from OpenStreetMap and, if you buy a pass, talking to Apple’s App Store. The stories, the texts and the photos are inside the app. The details, including the IP address that map requests let OpenStreetMap see, are in “Maps”.

Who we are and why we process data

Data controller: Carmelo Albanese, Terranova Sappo Minulio (RC), Italia.

For any question about privacy, write to supporto.arcotour@gmail.com.

Why we process data, and on what legal basis (Article 6 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the GDPR):

Your phone’s location, for the features that require it: with consent for the optional use of location features (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), based on an informed choice before activation. Technical permission in iOS does not by itself replace the GDPR requirements for consent. GPS coordinates are processed only on the phone and are not transmitted to the controller.

The maps and the Rome passes: insofar as necessary to provide the requested maps and content and manage purchased passes (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Loading maps involves requests to OpenStreetMap; purchases are handled by Apple.

The messages you write to us: to handle enquiries about purchased passes in performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR); for general enquiries, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support (point (f)). We do not use these messages for advertising.

Legal obligations: only where and to the extent that a law applicable to the controller requires the processing or retention of personal data (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR). In such cases the retention period follows the applicable legal obligation.

Who receives it: the app’s data does not reach us. OpenStreetMap (map requests) and Apple (purchases) receive it on their own behalf; the support mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail) and this website by GitHub.

Outside the European Union: Apple, Google and GitHub belong to groups based in the United States and, in their own policies, state that they use the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses for transfers of data outside the European Economic Area (GitHub also adheres to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework). The OpenStreetMap Foundation is registered in the United Kingdom, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of data protection. Where we use providers that transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area, the applicable safeguards depend on the specific service and relationship, including adequacy decisions and, where relevant, standard contractual clauses (Articles 45 and 46 GDPR). Transfers independently carried out by Apple and OpenStreetMap are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Children: ArcoTour has no accounts and does not ask the age of its users. The minimum age for using the app and the rules on children’s consent are being checked: they will be stated here before final publication.

Your location

ArcoTour lets you view maps and start tours without allowing access to your phone’s location.

Location access is optional. The app requests permission through iOS when you use a feature that needs it, such as route guidance or the “Use my location” button in the “Transport” section.

GPS coordinates stay on the device. To display the maps, OpenStreetMap receives the technical data of the request and the requested map area, which may approximately indicate the area where the user is located. The operator of ArcoTour does not receive the GPS coordinates, which are processed directly on your device. During guided tours, location access may continue while the screen is off or the app is in the background, allowing stories to start automatically.

If you deny permission, maps remain available. Guided navigation cannot follow your movements, and the “Transport” section displays stops in the tour area, clearly indicating that distances are not calculated from your location.

You can change or revoke location permission at any time in your iPhone’s Settings. Loading maps from OpenStreetMap is separate from GPS access: it happens even if you deny permission and involves requests to the service’s servers, which may receive your IP address and the requested map area (see “Maps”).

Maps

The maps come from OpenStreetMap. To show them, the app downloads small map tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The tiles come from a network of servers in several countries, which the OpenStreetMap Foundation uses.

Those servers receive your IP address, the tiles you ask for (that is, the area of the map you are looking at, which may roughly include where you are: for example when “Take me there” frames the route from your position to the stop), the time of the request and some technical information, such as the name of the app. They do not receive your name or your GPS coordinates.

OpenStreetMap may record this data: its own policy lists IP address, browser and device type, operating system, date and time and pages accessed, which it uses to run and protect the service and for anonymised statistics. It handles them on its own behalf: we neither receive nor see them. An IP address, however, is personal data. How long it keeps them is up to OpenStreetMap, not us; how it handles them is explained in its own policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.

For this reason, in the App Store privacy information we declare, as a precaution, “approximate location” as data linked to you, used only to make the maps work: we do not use it for advertising or to track you.

The app keeps a temporary copy of the tiles you have already seen on your phone, so you can see them again without internet.

Purchases

The Rome passes are bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple account: we neither see nor keep your card number or any other payment data, and we do not receive your name or the email of your Apple account. How Apple handles your data is explained in its own policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

The app receives the purchase confirmation from Apple and keeps on your phone only which passes you have and until when they are valid, to unlock the content. “Restore purchases” asks Apple to confirm your purchases again and needs internet.

What stays on your phone

The language you chose, the state of your passes (with a time reference to check when they expire) and a temporary copy of the map tiles. Nothing else.

This data stays on your phone until you remove the app: removing it deletes it. There is no archive on any server of ours.

What we do not do

There is no account to create. There are no ads. We do not use cookies, analytics, statistics or tracking tools, nor third-party libraries that collect data about how you use the app.

We do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.

We do not sell or hand over data: the app’s data does not reach us. The typefaces are inside the app: they are not downloaded from outside services.

Your rights

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) gives you the right to ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to have its processing restricted, to receive it in a portable format and to object to the processing. You can also withdraw at any time the permissions you have given, such as location, from iOS Settings.

The app has no accounts and its data does not reach us: the only personal data we may have is that of the message, if you write to us. We keep support messages for up to 24 months after the request is closed, unless the law requires us to keep them for longer. To exercise your rights write to supporto.arcotour@gmail.com: we reply without undue delay and in any case within one month of receiving the request, subject to any extensions provided for by the GDPR.

For the data that OpenStreetMap (map requests) and Apple (purchases) handle on their own behalf, you exercise your rights with them, using the contacts given in their own policies: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy and apple.com/legal/privacy.

If you think your data has been handled incorrectly you can write to us at supporto.arcotour@gmail.com and you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country (in Italy, the Garante: garanteprivacy.it).

Changes

If in the future the app uses data in a different way (for example with an account or with downloadable maps), we will update this page and the date below, and we will say so in the app before we start.

This website

Cookies, browsing data and email

This website does not use cookies, has no analytics or tracking tools and does not load fonts, images or scripts from other services: everything you see is hosted here.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). As GitHub explains, when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored by GitHub for security purposes, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account. We do not use this data. How GitHub handles it is explained in its privacy statement and on the page What is GitHub Pages?.

If you email us, we use your address and the content of your message only to reply to you. How long we keep them is explained in the “Your rights” section. The support mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail).
